GEAR PARAMETER VALUES AND SOURCES
Project: CAR wheg planetary gearbox, MATLAB model
Updated 2026-10-30. Same registry as the Obsidian note "06 - Sources".

Every value in main.m and the function files carries one of the IDs below in its comment (e.g. "% VDI2 Tab. 2"). Local files open straight from the links.

The table at the bottom ("Where each input comes from") lists every input with its source.


Project (N. Ramos)

== NR-geo
N. Ramos, gear design. Tooth counts (16 / 11 / 38, outer 38 / 50 / 50), module 1 mm, face width 10 mm, pressure angle 20°, helix 0°, profile shifts +0.40 / +0.15 / +0.70, 3 planets. Set in `main.m`; should match CAD.

== NR-cad
N. Ramos, robot design (CAD). Wheel radius closed 0.060 m and open 0.1029 m, body dimensions behind the frontal area (0.07 × 0.24 + 2 × 0.14 × 0.07 m).

== NR-motor
Drive motor datasheet, via N. Ramos. 12 V; no-load 170 rpm; loaded 145 rpm; stall 78 kg·cm (= 7.65 N·m); rated 11.5 kg·cm. ±10 % on speed. *Motor model not recorded yet; add the part number and datasheet link here.*

== NR-est
N. Ramos, project estimates and assumptions (in `main.m`, not measured): vehicle mass 8 kg; rolling resistance 0.10; grade 0°; drivetrain efficiency 0.94; ambient 25 °C; voltage fraction 1.0; ED 0.30; wear duty 50/50 closed/open, 3 h/day, 365 d, 5 yr; quality Q 9; all deviations and tolerances 0; PA12 Poisson ratio 0.47 (no source found, see FAES); steel ν 0.30 (see MIF304).

== PROJ
Project criteria, 2026-10-30. Framing set by N. Ramos: research prototype, short tests; the gears must survive static use and not melt or be badly affected by temperature; life is reference only. Specific thresholds chosen in the analysis to implement that: peak load = motor stall; flank static screen at first subsurface yield (JOH); test duty ED = 1; Tg = CAUTION, HDT = FAIL.

== VDI1
VDI 2736 Blatt 1 (2016). *Thermoplastic gear wheels — Materials, material selection, production methods, production tolerances, form design.* [Local PDF](file:///C:/Users/nico5/OneDrive/Documents/Textbooks/Engineering%20Resources/Vdi2736-Blatt-1.pdf). Fig. 5 p.19: pin-on-disc k_w and μ vs 100Cr6 (PA12 ≈ 16·10⁻⁶ mm³/(N·m), μ ≈ 0.27). Table 5 p.20: steel partner roughness for PA12, Rz 0.5–1.0 µm, HRC > 50.

== ISO6336-2
ISO 6336-2:2006, Calculation of load capacity of spur and helical gears, Part 2: surface durability. [Local PDF](file:///C:/Users/nico5/OneDrive/Documents/Textbooks/Engineering%20Resources/ISO-6336-2-2006%20%20-%20GEARS.pdf). Z_H, Eq. 16 (VDI2 p.16 fn. 10 allows the DIN 3990-2 form).

== KG
KG STOCK GEARS Technical Data (Kyouiku Gear). [Local PDF](file:///C:/Users/nico5/OneDrive/Documents/Textbooks/Engineering%20Resources/TechnicalData_KGSTOCKGEARS.pdf). PDF page numbers:
- p.11, Table 3: standard basic rack α 20°, h_aP 1.00m, h_fP 1.25m, c 0.25m, ρ_fP 0.38m, p = πm (tooth thickness πm/2 at the reference line).
- p.30: undercut limit x = (17 − z)/17 for 20°, equal to 1 − z·sin²α/2; working pressure angle inv α_w = 2·tan α·(x₁ + x₂)/(z₁ + z₂) + inv α.
- p.117, §9.9: planetary ratio with the ring fixed, 1 + z_r/z_s; assembly condition (z_s + z_r)/N = integer; closure z_r = z_s + 2·z_p.

== DYK
Doğan, O., Yüce, C., Karpat, F. (2021). *A Novel Method for Tooth Bending Stress Calculation of Gears with Asymmetric Teeth.* Research Square preprint. [doi:10.21203/rs.3.rs-758867/v1](https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-758867/v1) · [Local PDF](file:///C:/Users/nico5/OneDrive/Documents/Textbooks/Engineering%20Resources/DIN%203390%20Eqs.pdf) (filed as "DIN 3390 Eqs"). Gives the DIN 3990 Method C forms: Y_Fa Eq. 3, Y_Sa Eq. 4 (1.2 + 0.13·L)·q_s^(1/(1.21 + 2.3/L)), Y_ε Eq. 5.


Materials

== HP-DS
HP 3D High Reusability PA 12 datasheet, doc. 4AA6-4895ENA, March 2018. [PDF (Shapeways copy)](https://www.shapeways.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/HP-MJF-PA-12-Data-sheet.pdf). Tensile strength 48 MPa XY/Z (ASTM D638); tensile modulus 1800 MPa XY/Z; flexural strength 65 XY / 70 Z MPa (ASTM D790); HDT 175 °C at 0.45 MPa, 95 °C at 1.82 MPa (ASTM D648); powder melting point 187 °C (ASTM D3418). Typical values, not for specification.

== HP-TFS
HP 3D HR PA 12 Materials Technical Fact Sheet, balanced print mode, doc. 4AA7-1534EEW, Nov 2017. [PDF](https://erpro-group.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/10/HP-3D-HR-PA12-Extended-datasheet.pdf). Tensile modulus 1700 MPa XY, 1800 MPa Z (ASTM D638). Note: a second table in the same sheet gives HDT 1.82 MPa XY as 80 °C (vs 95 °C on p.1).

== OD20
O'Connor, H.J., Dowling, D.P. (2020). *Comparison between the properties of polyamide 12 and glass bead filled polyamide 12 using the multi jet fusion printing process.* Additive Manufacturing 31. [doi:10.1016/j.addma.2019.100961](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.addma.2019.100961) · [UCD open copy](https://researchrepository.ucd.ie/rest/bitstreams/46424/retrieve). MJF PA12: Tg 44 °C (DSC, Table 5); tensile strength 47/48/49 MPa X/Y/Z (Table 6).

== FAES
Faes, M., Wang, Y., Lava, P., Moens, D. *Variability in the Mechanical Properties of Laser Sintered PA-12 Components.* Solid Freeform Fabrication Symposium 2015. [PDF](https://utw10945.utweb.utexas.edu/sites/default/files/2015/2015-69-Faes.pdf). Poisson's ratio LS PA12 0.40 (DIC, ASTM D638; 0.387–0.409 by orientation).

== NAR23
Nar, K., Majewski, C., Lewis, R. (2023). *Evaluating the effect of solid lubricant inclusion on the friction and wear properties of Laser Sintered Polyamide-12 components.* Wear 522, 204873. [doi:10.1016/j.wear.2023.204873](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.wear.2023.204873) · [White Rose open copy](https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/id/eprint/202329/7/1-s2.0-S0043164823002569-main.pdf). Neat LS PA12 vs stainless ball, 0.35 m/s: k_w 4.78·10⁻⁴ (10 N) and 3.20·10⁻⁴ (30 N) mm³/(N·m), μ 0.31 / 0.26 (Table 5).

== ODR
Odrobina, M. *Polimer fogaskerékpárok tribológiai viselkedésének értékelése hagyományos és XCT technológiával* (Tribological evaluation of polymer gear pairs with conventional and XCT methods). PhD thesis, MATE. [Repository](https://phd.mater.uni-mate.hu/409) · [Thesis PDF](https://phd.mater.uni-mate.hu/409/11/odrobina_miklos_thesis_DOI.pdf). SLS PA12/PA12 dry gears: k_w 56–512·10⁻⁶ mm³/(N·m) (284 = midpoint), at pv 9.2–11.03 MPa·m/s.

== TA
TA Instruments, Application Note EF037. *Tensile and Fatigue Properties of Additively Manufactured Polyamides.* [PDF](https://www.tainstruments.com/pdf/literature/EF037.pdf). SLS PA12 dog-bones, R = 0.1, 25 Hz: 25 MPa ran out at 10⁷ cycles.

== TN304
Thomasnet, "All About 304 Steel". [Article](https://www.thomasnet.com/articles/metals-metal-products/all-about-304-steel-properties-strength-and-uses/). 304 stainless: yield 205 MPa, UTS 515 MPa, E 193–200 GPa. The model assumes the planets are 304; the grade isn't confirmed yet.

== MIF304
MakeItFrom, Annealed 304 Stainless Steel. [Page](https://www.makeitfrom.com/material-properties/Annealed-304-Stainless-Steel). Poisson's ratio 0.28 (the model's 0.30 is NR-est).


Physics and constants

== JOH
Johnson, K.L. (1985). *Contact Mechanics.* Cambridge University Press. ISBN 978-0-521-34796-9. [Catalogue](https://ci.nii.ac.jp/ncid/BA00227815?l=en). Line (cylinder) contact: max shear 0.30·p₀ below the surface, so Tresca first yield at p₀ = 3.3k = 1.67·Y.

== USSA76
U.S. Standard Atmosphere, 1976 (COESA). [Definitions (PDAS)](https://www.pdas.com/atmosdef.html). Sea-level density 1.225 kg/m³.

== GN
Standard gravity g_n = 9.80665 m/s² (3rd CGPM, 1901). [Reference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Standard_gravity). The model rounds to 9.81.
